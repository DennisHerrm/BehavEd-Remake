Tutorials: missions and models

Give NPCs paths to walk

Create the NPC waypoint network automatically, check it, and add paths by hand.

步骤#

  1. 1

    Open the window. NPC 路径点 on the map toolbar. 显示路网 shows the network: green by hand, blue automatic, orange jumps.

  2. 2

    Create automatically. Press 生成路网: waypoints are spread over the walkable floor and connected. The spacing slider sets how dense (the original levels use about 230).

  3. 3

    Check. 检查路网 finds what the game would reject - click a problem to fly there.

  4. 4

    By hand. 放置路径点: click the floor to place a path; 连接 connects two points (Ctrl: a jump).
