Tutorials: missions and models

Look at a character model

Load any character of the game, turn it, look at its skins and its skeleton.

Schritte#

  1. 1

    Switch to Modell in the mode bar.

  2. 2

    Pick a character. NPCs lists every character of the game - type to search, click to load it with its skin.

  3. 3

    Look around. Left mouse turns, middle mouse moves, the wheel zooms. Drehteller turns it by itself, Ansicht einpassen brings it back to the front.

  4. 4

    Details. Haut switches skins, Knochen shows the skeleton, Texturen switches textures off.
