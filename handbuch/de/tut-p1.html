1 · Create the mission

A new mission on taspir1: map, player start, script runner and an intro script are made for you.

Schritte#

  1. 1

    Open the start page. Switch the mode bar to Mission. Without a map, the tab shows the start page.

  2. 2

    New mission. Click Neue Mission erstellen. In the list type taspir1 and pick it. As Missionsname type taspir_tut - letters, digits and _. The Skriptordner is filled in by itself. Press Anlegen.

  3. 3

    Look at what you got. The map opens on the landing pad. behaved has added a target_scriptrunner next to the player start: when the player spawns, the script taspir_tut/intro runs. It is open in a tab - a short camera shot behind the player start.

  4. 4

    Switch off the original intro. taspir1 has its own start: its player start already triggers a runner with taspir1/preIntro, and the same trigger now starts yours too - both would play at once. Type t271 into the search box of the entity list - that is the runner of the original (its usescript is taspir1/preIntro) - select it and press Delete. The Graph view shows such connections at a glance (Graph: was startet was).

Every map of the game works this way. Its own entities stay; remove what you do not want - it changes only your mission, never the game.
