リファレンス

Every button

Each button of the program - the mode bar, the action column, the map toolbar, the placement tiles, the inspector, the camera, the timeline and the model view - with what it does.

Hover over any button in the program to see the same explanation as a tooltip. Buttons with a coloured frame are switches: on or off.

The mode bar#

The strip at the far left switches what the window shows. The script stays the same in every mode.

ModeWhat it shows
イベントThe イベント list of every command and macro, the script tree, the action column - for writing scripts. スクリプトを書く
ミッションThe mission: the entity list and inspector, the map in 3D with the timeline below - for cameras, figures, entities and saving the mission. マップ
モデルA character model with its animations - to find an animation and put it into the script. モデルビューとキャラクター

The action column (Events mode)#

ButtonWhat it does
削除Removes the selected lines (Delete).
複製Inserts a copy right below (Space).
コピー / 切り取り / 貼り付けClipboard (Ctrl+C / X / V). Paste goes below the selection; text from other programs is accepted after a question.
コメント化Turns lines into comments and back (Backspace).
検索The Find window (Ctrl+F): search by command or value, replace in one or all scripts. Find and replace in all open scripts
上へ / 下へMoves the selection one line, inside its block (Alt+Up/Down).
元に戻す / やり直しUndo and redo. Right-click either one for the full history. Undo anything - even one step from the middle
新規 / 開くAn empty script / open a .txt or a compiled .ibi.
最近使用Recently opened files (Alt+M).
追加読み込みAdds another script to the end of this one.
保存 / すべて保存 / 名前を付けて保存Save this script, every changed tab, or under a new name.
エクスポートWrites the script as plain text and opens it.
バックアップ / 復元Keeps a copy next to the script (.bak) / goes back to it (Alt+B / Alt+R).
型を表示Shows the value types in the tree.
%g 浮動小数Shows numbers short: 1000 instead of 1000.000.
マクロを畳むShows a macro as one line.
+ / -Expands or collapses every block.
コンパイル！Saves the script and writes the .ibi the game runs; green when it worked, red with the messages when not. Compile and fix errors

The map toolbar (Mission mode)#

ButtonWhat it does
新規ミッションStarts a mission on any map: player start, script runner, intro script and mission folder are created. 1 · Create the mission
ミッションを読み込む…Opens a mission from a .pk3: map, entities, every script in tabs, the figures. ミッション
ミッションを保存Saves every script, writes the .ent, compiles and packs the .pk3 - after checking the mission. 8 · Save and play
ミッションなしThe name of the open mission; a dot means unsaved changes. Hover for its folder.
マップとファイルOpens a map from disk or from a .pk3, a mission folder, or a separate .ent file.
スクリプトThe scripts the map's entities point to - pick one to open it.
エンティティShows the entity markers: walk targets, marks, runners, spawners.
名前Writes the names of entities and figures into the view.
キャラクターShows the script's figures, moved by the timeline.
エフェクトShows the effects of the map (fx_runner).
グローThe glow of shaders marked glow, as r_DynamicGlow.
動的ライトLightsabers and effect lights light up walls and figures.
霧The fog of the map, as the game draws it.
空The sky; switched off you can look into the map from outside.
カメラ視点Shows what the camera sees - the selected camera command, or the camera at the playhead. Look through the camera while you work
トップビューLooks straight down; a cut height hides ceilings. Look at the map from above
NPC ウェイポイントThe NPC waypoint window: show, create and check the network. NPC ウェイポイント
グリッドSnaps entities to 8 to 64 units while dragging and placing.
表示設定Brightness, ambient light, detail, shadows, highlighting the active script's entities. See better in dark maps
統計Frame rate, textures, triangles and graphics card values.

The placement tiles#

The strip on the left edge of the map. Click a tile, then click on the floor - or drag the tile into the view. Shift places several, Esc stops. Place, move and turn entities

ButtonWhat it does
キャラクター (NPC_spawner)A character that spawns: set NPC_type and a name.
移動先 (waypoint_navgoal)A place a figure walks to (SET_NAVGOAL).
カメラ/注視マーク (ref_tag)A mark with a direction - for cameras (tag) and look targets.
注視先 (target_position)A point to look at or to aim at.
スクリプトランナー (target_scriptrunner)Runs a script when it is used - by a trigger, the player start or another script.
エフェクト (fx_runner)Plays an effect file (.efx).
サウンド (target_speaker)Plays a sound, once or in a loop.
NPC ウェイポイント (waypoint)A point of the NPC navigation network.
プレイヤー開始位置 (info_player_start)Where the player spawns.

The entity inspector#

ButtonWhat it does
そこへ移動Flies the view to the entity.
床に置くPuts it on the floor below, like DROPTOFLOOR in the game.
複製A copy - of every selected entity. Alt+drag in the view does the same.
削除Removes it.
アニメートOpens the Animation window for doors, platforms, ships and effects. 可動物のアニメーションと ROFF ファイル
元に戻すUndoes the last entity change (entities have their own history).
やり直しRedoes it.
すべてのキー (生)Shows every key as plain text.
+ キーAdds a key.
開くNext to a script key: opens the script - and creates it in the mission folder if it does not exist yet.

The camera bar#

Appears in the view when the camera is selected. カメラ

ButtonWhat it does
移動Arrows: move the shot along one axis, the box in the middle in the view plane.
回転Rings: turn the shot.
ワールドWorld or Local: the arrows follow the map axes or the camera.
前のショットThe previous shot.
次のショットThe next shot.
ビューに合わせるPuts the shot where you are looking.
操縦Sit in the shot and fly it; Enter sets a key. Fly a camera shot yourself
カメラ視点Look through the camera.
カメラプレビューA small picture of the camera's view in the corner.
ここに新しいショットA new shot at your view, after the selected one.
ショットを削除Removes the shot.

The timeline#

ButtonWhat it does
先頭へBack to the start of the script.
再生Plays the scene - camera, figures, sound - in game time.
ループStarts again at the end.
サウンドPlays the sounds while the timeline runs.
追従Moves the view along with the camera.
ズームアウトShows more time.
ズームインShows less time, in more detail.
キャラクター行One row per figure: when each one walks, speaks and acts.
動きのないトラックAlso shows rows where nothing happens.
waitShows the waits as their own blocks.
ここにカメラを挿入Inserts a camera shot at the playhead.

The time display switches between seconds and frames when you click it; the list next to the play button sets the speed. Drag blocks to move them in time - タイムライン.
