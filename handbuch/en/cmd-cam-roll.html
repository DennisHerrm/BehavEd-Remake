Commands

camera ROLL - tilt the picture

Rolls the camera to an angle.

View:

What it does#

Tilts the picture to the side - for a dizzy or dramatic shot.

Where to enter what#

No.NameWhat it does
1Camera commandThe first field chooses what the camera does - here ROLL. Changing it changes the fields below.
2AngleThe roll angle the camera ends at (not added to the current roll).
3TimeMilliseconds; with a time it takes the shorter way there.

Example#

camera ( ROLL, 15, 1000 ).

In Classic, Graph and Tracks#

The switch at the top of the page chooses the view. All three work on the same script - switching loses nothing.

In Classic it is one line of the script tree. Double-click it (or select it and press Enter) to open the editor; right-click for the menu; drag it to move it.

In Graph the script is a node; select it and its commands are listed on the right under Commands of this script. Double-click the line to edit it, right-click for the menu, drop a command from the Events list on it to insert after it.

In Tracks the camera row shows the shots - from one MOVE to the next. This command is listed in the Command list; the timeline under the map shows it in its own camera row.
