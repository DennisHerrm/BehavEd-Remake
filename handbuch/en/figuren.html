Map and scene

Figures and paths

Figures walk to their navgoals and ships fly their moves - you see the whole path and can change it on the map.

Selecting figures and ships#

Click a figure or a ship in the view to select its entity - the spawner of the figure, the brush entity of the ship. Marker and gizmo follow the model while it moves, and an outline shows what is selected. The arrow at the figure points where the model looks.

Start, end and new points#

  • Drag the end handle to move the walk target (the waypoint_navgoal) or the end of a flight.
  • Drag the start handle to move only the start, not the whole line.
  • Grab the figure in the middle of its walk (timeline between start and arrival) and drag: a new point is created at that moment - a new navgoal for figures, a split move for ships.
  • Rotate works the same way: in the middle of a flight the rings create a new point with the new angles; for a figure they write set ( SET_ANGLES ) at that time - while walking it applies when the figure arrives, because the game turns walking figures toward their direction.

Walk or run, shared targets#

Right-click the end handle of a walk (or its band in the timeline) for its menu:

  • Walk (from this walk on) / Run (from this walk on) - writes SET_WALKING or SET_RUNNING before the navgoal.
  • Look at while walking - the figure keeps turning toward another figure while it walks (SET_WATCHTARGET).
  • Walk backwards (keeps looking back) - a look mark behind the start makes it walk backwards; Face the walking direction ends that.

When several figures share one navgoal, moving it would move all of them. BehavEd-Remake notices and gives this walk its own copy of the target first - the status line tells you which one it created.

Walking backwards is detected in existing scripts too: when a figure moves against the way it faces, the scene plays BOTH_WALKBACK1 or BOTH_RUNBACK1 like the game.
