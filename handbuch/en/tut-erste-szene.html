A first cutscene

Two camera shots, a smooth move between them, watched through the camera and compiled for the game.

Steps#

  1. 1

    Open the mission. File → Load mission, choose MD_Missions_Ep3.pk3, pick md_am_sith. The map opens with all scripts of the mission in tabs; stay in the tab intro2_sith.

  2. 2

    Select the camera. Click the camera symbol on the map (or the Camera row of the timeline). Its shots appear in the list on the left, its path on the map.

  3. 3

    Fly to a good spot and add a shot. Hold the right mouse button and fly with W A S D until you like the view. Press New camera shot here: a camera ( MOVE ) and camera ( PAN ) at your view are added after the selected shot.

  4. 4

    Give it a travel time. Under Selected shot set Travel time to 3 (seconds): the camera now flies three seconds from the previous shot to this one. 0 would be a cut.

  5. 5

    Watch it. Turn on Camera preview and click the small picture to look through the camera, then press the play button (▶) under the map. The timeline plays the scene as the game will - with sound when Sound is on.

  6. 6

    Save and compile. A script from a .pk3 has no file yet, so the first save asks where to put it: choose the scripts folder of your mod (for example MD\scripts\md_am\). Compile! then writes the .ibi the game runs next to it. For a mission of your own, Save mission packs everything into a .pk3 - see Your own mission.

What next#

Grab the camera gizmo between two shots to set a key there (Moving and turning a shot), or fly a whole shot yourself with Pilot (Fly a shot yourself (Pilot)).
