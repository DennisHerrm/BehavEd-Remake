Welcome

BehavEd-Remake writes the scripts that run cutscenes and missions in Jedi Academy and Movie Duels - and shows them on the map while you write them.

The game runs its scenes with ICARUS scripts: lists of commands such as "walk to this spot", "play this sound", "move the camera there". Raven's original BehavEd let you build those lists from buttons. BehavEd-Remake does the same, file for file compatible, and adds what the original never had: the map in 3D, the figures walking their paths, the camera flying its shots, and a timeline that plays it all - before you ever start the game.

Where to start#

  • New here? Read Getting started and The window - ten minutes, and every later page makes sense.
  • Want to make something right away? Pick one of the tutorials below.
  • Looking for one thing? Type it into the search box at the top left - it searches every page and jumps to the spot.

Tutorials#

Your own mission: take-off on Taspir

A complete little mission for Jedi Academy, built from nothing: Kyle and Rosh walk across a landing pad, the camera follows them, the Raven's Claw lifts off and flies past the camera, the picture shakes and fades to black - and the game plays it.

Eight parts · base game · about 30 minutes

Besides the big mission there are 63 short tutorials - each shows one thing in three to six steps. All of them, with pictures: Tutorials.

What the program can do#

AreaIn short
Writing scriptsBuild scripts from the command list, edit every value in a dialog, copy, move, undo - like the original BehavEd.
Script Flow: Classic, Graph, TracksSee a script as a tree, as a graph of who starts whom, or as tracks over time.
The mapThe map in 3D with lights, effects and models; fly through it, select and move entities.
CameraCamera shots as keys on the map: move and turn them with the gizmo, fly them yourself, preview them.
TimelinePlay the scene, see every command in time, drag commands to change when they happen.
Figures and pathsPaths of walking figures and flying ships: start and end handles, new points, walk or run, facing.
Animating movers and ROFF filesAnimate ships, doors and platforms - as move commands or as a ROFF file, built and edited right here.
MissionsOpen a whole mission with all its scripts, build your own, and pack it into a .pk3 the game loads.
Nothing is lost. Every change can be undone with Ctrl+Z, and files are only written when you save. The game files themselves are never changed: new things go next to your scripts or into your own .pk3.
