    Model view and characters

    Look at any character of the game with its skins and animations, and put an animation into the script with a double-click.

    Switch to Model in the mode bar. NPCs lists every character of the game (from the .npc files) - click one to load its model and skin as the game shows it. Open .glm and From .pk3 open any model. Left mouse orbits, middle mouse pans, the wheel zooms; Turntable, Bones and Fit view sit in the toolbar.

    The Animations list on the left shows every animation of the skeleton with its length; filter by name or by BOTH / TORSO / LEGS / FACE. Click plays it; double-click (or Into script) puts set ( SET_ANIM_BOTH, "…" ) after the selected line of the open script - SET_ANIM_UPPER for TORSO and SET_ANIM_LOWER for LEGS animations.
