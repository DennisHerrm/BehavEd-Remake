Script Flow: Classic, Graph, Tracks

Three views of the same script - switch any time, nothing is lost.

The buttons Classic / Graph / Tracks at the top of the Script Flow column switch the view. In a split view every pane has its own.

Classic#

The tree of the original BehavEd - every command one line, blocks to fold open. This is where you edit; see Writing scripts.

Graph: what starts what#

A mission is held together by its entities: a trigger targets a target_scriptrunner, which runs a script, which affects figures and starts other scripts. The graph draws exactly that. Double-click a node to open its script or show the entity in the map; drag from the dot on the right of a node to another node to connect them - for a figure you choose what kind of script (spawnscript, deathscript, ...). Drag entities from the list or commands into the graph; a command dropped on free space creates a new script.

Fit shows everything, Arrange sorts the nodes, Right angles draws the wires at right angles through the gaps. Double-click a wire for a bend point; right-click removes it.

Tracks: the script over time#

A compact timeline inside the script column: rows for Camera, every figure, Dialogue (voice lines) and Script. Click a block to select its line, drag its right edge to change the duration, drag in the ruler to move through time. The big Timeline under the map does the same with more detail.
