Tutorials: scripts

Move, copy and comment out lines

Rearrange a script: select several lines, drag them, duplicate them, and switch lines off without deleting them.

View:

Steps#

  1. 1

    Select. Click a line in the tree. Ctrl+click adds more lines, Shift+click selects everything between. Every button of the action column works on all selected lines at once.

  2. 2

    Move. Drag the selection to a new place - or press Move up / Move down in the action column (Alt+Up / Alt+Down) to move it one line. It stays inside its block. A block moves with everything inside it.

  3. 3

    Duplicate. Duplicate (Space) inserts a copy of the selection right below - the quickest way to a second camera shot or a second walk.

  4. 4

    Copy between scripts. Copy (Ctrl+C) copies the selection. Switch to another tab, select a line and press Paste (Ctrl+V): the commands are inserted below that line.

  5. 5

    Switch off without deleting. REM (Backspace) turns the selected lines into comments: they stay in the script, but the game skips them. Press it again (or Ctrl+Backspace) to switch them back on.

  1. 1

    Select. Click a line under Commands of this script. The action column on the right works on it, as in Classic.

  2. 2

    Right-click for the rest. The menu of a line has everything: Edit, Duplicate, Copy, Cut, Paste, Move up, Move down, REM and Delete.

    No.NameWhat it does
    1Edit...Opens the event editor.
    2DuplicateA copy right below.
    3Copy / Cut / PasteThrough the clipboard - also into another script.
    4Move up / Move downOne line up or down, inside its block.
    5REMSwitches the line off (comment) or on again.
    6DeleteRemoves the line.
  3. 3

    Or the action column. Move up / Move down, Duplicate, Copy, Paste and REM on the right do the same for the selected line.

  1. 1

    Select. Click a block in the timeline - or a line of the command list.

  2. 2

    Right-click for the rest. The menu of a block has Edit, Duplicate, Copy, Cut, Paste, Move up, Move down, REM and Delete.

    No.NameWhat it does
    1Edit...Opens the event editor.
    2DuplicateA copy right after it - on the timeline it follows the first.
    3Copy / Cut / PasteThrough the clipboard.
    4Move up / Move downOne line up or down in the script - that changes the order in time.
    5REMSwitches the command off or on.
    6DeleteRemoves it.
  3. 3

    Moving changes the time. Moving a wait up or down shifts everything after it - watch the blocks move.

Good to know#

  • Delete (Delete) removes lines for good - but Ctrl+Z brings them back.
