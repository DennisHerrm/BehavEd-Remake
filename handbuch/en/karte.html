Map and scene

The map

The map in 3D, drawn like the game draws it - with lights, glow, fog, effects and the figures of the script.

Opening a map#

Switch to Mission in the mode bar. A mission brings its map along (Missions). Without a mission, Maps files opens a map from disk or from a .pk3, or loads a separate .ent file. Scripts (N) lists the scripts the map's entities point to - pick one to load its cutscene.

Moving around#

W A S D (mouse over the view)Fly; Q / E down and up, Shift faster
Right mouse dragLook around
Middle mouse dragPan
Alt + middle mouseOrbit
WheelZoom
Double-click an entity in the listFly there
Ctrl + 1 ... 9Save the view; 1 ... 9 jumps back (kept per map)

What is shown#

The buttons on the right of the toolbar switch parts of the picture on and off: entities, names, the figures of the script, effects, glow, dynamic light, fog and sky. Top view looks straight down like a map and cuts away everything above the cut height - drag the height box to look at another floor. View settings (gear) holds brightness, ambient light, detail, character shadows and Highlight this script's entities, which dims every entity the active script does not use.

The picture is meant to look like the game: shaders, lightmaps, glow (like r_DynamicGlow), fog (r_drawfog), dynamic lights, lens flares, weather and saber trails. Statistics shows frame rate, textures and graphics card values when something looks wrong.
