2 · Place the characters

Kyle and Rosh at the south edge of the pad, and a walk target in front of the ship for each of them.

Steps#

  1. 1

    Look at the pad. Hold the right mouse button and fly with W A S D until you see the pad from the south, a little from above.

  2. 2

    Place Kyle. Click the tile Character (NPC_spawner) on the left edge of the view, then click near the south edge of the pad. A character marker appears and is selected.

  3. 3

    Name him. In the inspector under the entity list set Name to kyle and NPC_type to Kyle (the ... button lists every character of the game). Onto floor puts him exactly on the ground.

    No.NameWhat it does
    1Fly thereFlies the view to the entity.
    2Onto floorPuts it exactly on the floor below.
    3Duplicate / DeleteCopies or removes it - every selected entity.
    4Undo / RedoThe history of entity changes, separate from the script's.
    5All keys (raw)Shows every key as plain text.
    6Filter fieldsShows only the keys whose name contains the text.
    7...Next to NPC_type: searches every character of the game.
  4. 4

    Place Rosh the same way beside him: Name rosh, NPC_type rosh_penin.

  5. 5

    Two walk targets. Click the tile Walk target (waypoint_navgoal) and click in front of the ship - name it nav_kyle. A second one beside it: nav_rosh.

Hold Shift while clicking to place several entities of one kind. More in Placing new entities.
